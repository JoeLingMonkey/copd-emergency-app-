ASSESSMENT

If unconscious, gasping, or severely struggling to breathe, call emergency services immediately.

  1. Is the person unconscious or not responding?
  2. Are they gasping for air or severely struggling to breathe?
  3. Are their lips, face or fingernails blue or grey?
  4. Are they suddenly confused, unusually drowsy or difficult to wake?
  5. Do they have severe chest pain, collapse, or suddenly become cold and sweaty?
  6. Is their breathing getting rapidly worse, or is their prescribed rescue treatment not helping?

If ANY answer is YES, call emergency services immediately.

NO opens the main menu; it does not mean medical help is unnecessary.
